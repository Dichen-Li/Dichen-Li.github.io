About

I'm Dichen Li, an ECE M.S. student at UC San Diego (2024-2026). My research interests are cross-embodiment learning and locomanipulation across humanoid, quadruped, and hexapod robot platforms. I conduct research on cross-embodiment learning at UC San Diego under the guidance of Prof. Hao Su; previously, I visited UC Berkeley, working on hexapod robot locomanipulation under the guidance of Prof. Avideh Zakhor. I revieved my Bachelor's degree in Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University.
