About

I'm Dichen Li, an M.S. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UC San Diego (2024-2026). My research focuses on cross-embodiment learning and locomanipulation across humanoid, quadruped, and hexapod robots. I work under the guidance of Prof. Hao Su at UC San Diego. I previously visited UC Berkeley, where I worked with Prof. Avideh Zakhor on hexapod robot locomanipulation. I received my B.S. in Automation from Xi'an Jiaotong University graduating with honor.
